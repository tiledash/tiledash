# English (source language)

The English is the source: the other languages are translated from it, so write it plainly.

- Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and menu items ("Add a dashboard", not "Add A Dashboard"). Product names keep their capitals (Homey, Tiledash).
- Short and direct. Buttons are a verb or a verb and a noun ("Save", "Add tile"), without a full stop. Sentences in help text end with one.
- Address the reader as "you". Say what happened and what to do next.
- Typographic quotes (“ ”) and apostrophes (’) in running text.
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats), not written into the text; pass them in as values.
- Use the glossary's words ("device", "dashboard", "tile").
