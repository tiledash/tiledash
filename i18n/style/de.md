# Deutsch

Address: formal, **Sie/Ihr** (as in the Homey app; confirmed 2026-10-16).

- Write like Homey's own app in German: clear, friendly, factual. No stiff officialese.
- Sentence case as German spells it: nouns have a capital, headings and buttons otherwise only the first word ("Sprache und Region").
- Quotes: „…“ for running text (low opening, high closing). Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis.
- Punctuation as in German: no space before `: ; ! ?`. Buttons and headings have no full stop.
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: short imperative or noun ("Speichern", "Hinzufügen"). German words run long: watch every maxLength and prefer the shorter word.
