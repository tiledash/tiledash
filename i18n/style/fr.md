# Français

Address: formal, **vous** (as in the Homey app; confirmed 2026-10-16).

- Write like Homey's own app in French: clear, friendly, plain. Not stiff.
- Sentence case: only the first word and proper names have a capital ("Langue et région"). Days and months are lower case.
- Quotes: « … » for running text, with a narrow no-break space (U+202F) inside each guillemet (`« texte »`). Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis.
- Punctuation as in French: put a narrow no-break space (U+202F) before `: ; ! ?` (and before `»`, after `«`). Buttons and headings have no full stop. Example: `Enregistrer :` and `Vraiment ?` (the space is the one character U+202F).
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: short, a verb in the infinitive ("Enregistrer", "Ajouter") or a short noun. French runs long: respect every maxLength.
