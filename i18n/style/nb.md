# Norsk bokmål

Address: informal, **du/din** (as in the Homey app and almost all Norwegian software; confirmed 2026-11-08).

- Write in Bokmål, never Nynorsk, like Homey's own app in Norwegian: friendly, plain, short. Not stiff and not slangy.
- Sentence case everywhere: only the first word and proper names have a capital ("Språk og region").
- Quotes: «…» for running text. Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis, as the English does.
- Punctuation as in Norwegian: no space before `: ; ! ?`. Buttons and headings have no full stop.
- Compounds are written as one word ("veggpanel", "delingslenke"); with a product name or abbreviation use a hyphen ("Homey-enhet", "KI-assistent").
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: as short as Norwegian allows, a verb in the imperative ("Lagre", "Legg til") or a short noun. Respect every maxLength.
