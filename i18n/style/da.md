# Dansk

Address: informal, **du/din** (as in the Homey app and almost all Danish software; confirmed 2026-11-08).

- Write like Homey's own app in Danish: friendly, plain, short. Not stiff and not slangy.
- Sentence case everywhere: only the first word and proper names have a capital ("Sprog og område").
- Quotes: »…« for running text. Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis, as the English does.
- Punctuation as in Danish: no space before `: ; ! ?`. Buttons and headings have no full stop. Leave out the grammatical comma before a subordinate clause (the "new comma" style) where it reads easier.
- Compounds are written as one word ("vægpanel", "delingslink"); with a product name or abbreviation use a hyphen ("Homey-enhed", "AI-assistent").
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: as short as Danish allows, a verb in the imperative ("Gem", "Tilføj") or a short noun. Respect every maxLength.
