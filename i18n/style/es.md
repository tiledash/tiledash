# Español

Address: informal, **tú** (as in the Homey app; confirmed 2026-10-16). Neutral Spanish, not specific to Spain or Latin America.

- Write like Homey's own app in Spanish: friendly, plain, short.
- Sentence case: only the first word and proper names have a capital ("Idioma y región"). Days and months are lower case.
- Quotes: «…» for running text. Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis. Questions and exclamations open with ¿ and ¡.
- Punctuation as in Spanish: no space before `: ; ! ?`. Buttons and headings have no full stop.
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: short, a verb in the infinitive ("Guardar", "Añadir") or a short noun. Respect every maxLength.
