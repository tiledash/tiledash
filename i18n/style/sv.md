# Svenska

Address: informal, **du/din** (as in the Homey app and almost all Swedish software; confirmed 2026-11-08).

- Write like Homey's own app in Swedish: friendly, plain, short. Not stiff and not slangy.
- Sentence case everywhere: only the first word and proper names have a capital ("Språk och region").
- Quotes: ”…” for running text (the same mark on both sides). Use ’ for an apostrophe and … for an ellipsis, as the English does.
- Punctuation as in Swedish: no space before `: ; ! ?`. Buttons and headings have no full stop.
- Compounds are written as one word ("väggpanel", "delningslänk"); with a product name or abbreviation use a hyphen ("Homey-enhet", "AI-assistent").
- Numbers, units, dates and times are formatted by the app (Formats); never write a separator or unit spacing into the text yourself.
- Product names stay as they are (Homey, Flow, Advanced Flow, Insights, Tiledash, Pro, Free); other words follow the glossary.
- Buttons and badges: as short as Swedish allows, a verb in the imperative ("Spara", "Lägg till") or a short noun. Respect every maxLength.
